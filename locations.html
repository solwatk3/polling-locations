📥 Bulk Import Locations

Paste a block of location data and the app will parse it into individual entries for review.

Works with three formats: Block format (Name / Address / City, TN Zip, one blank line between entries — even from a spreadsheet with trailing tabs); Precinct format (rows starting with a number like 04-08 or 5); or tab-separated (Name → Address → City → Zip) copied from a spreadsheet. Each location needs a name, street address, city, and zip. The app will flag anything it can't fully parse.
